I recently purchased the "SoundWave Pro X" wireless headphones and have been
using them for about two weeks now. The battery life is incredible - I get
about 40 hours on a single charge. The noise cancellation is top-notch and
blocks out most office noise. The sound quality is crisp with deep bass.
However, the ear cushions could be more comfortable for extended use, and
the carrying case feels a bit cheap. I'd rate them 4.5/5 stars overall.
They retail for $179.99 and are well worth the price.
